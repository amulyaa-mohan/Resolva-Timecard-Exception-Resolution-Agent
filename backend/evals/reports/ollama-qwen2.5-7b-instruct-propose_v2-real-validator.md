## Limitations

- Real validator: `track-a attendance-rules@0.4-draft (make_validator + eval totals)`, Track A's attendance rules with trusted eval totals (`evals/aggregates.py`). Until Track A adopts `evidence_ref`, it accepts only the exact scheduled time, so a correct fix anchored on a note's stated time is blocked.
- Stand-in: `stub-window-v1 (stand-in until A-02)` checks the inference window and pairing only; it is shown for comparison and not scored on rules-only cases (breaks, totals).
- Eval cases carry complete evidence by default: an observed meal break, weekly/quarterly totals for the policy week and quarter, and an evaluation time after the shift.
- Proposal cases cover MISSING_PUNCH, the first end-to-end milestone. Other codes are covered by retrieval cases until Track A's detectors and validator exist.
- With the mock provider the proposal numbers test the harness, not model quality.
- Small sample (45 proposal cases): treat percentages as indicative, not statistically precise.
- Real-model outputs vary between runs even at temperature 0.1; a rare failure can appear in one run and not another. Safety rests on the deterministic gates, not on a lucky run.
