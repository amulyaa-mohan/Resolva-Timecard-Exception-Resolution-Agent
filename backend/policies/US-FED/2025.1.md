# US-FED Attendance Policy (with California overlay), version 2025.1

Simplified demonstration handbook for Resolva. Not legal advice.

## [US-1] Scope
This policy applies to non-exempt hourly employees at US locations. It governs how attendance is recorded, how hours are counted, and how timecard corrections are approved.

## [US-2] Weekly overtime
Hours worked beyond 40 hours in a workweek are overtime. Overtime is paid at one and one-half times the regular rate of pay.

## [US-3] Daily overtime (California overlay)
At locations covered by the California overlay, hours worked beyond 8 hours in a workday are also overtime. Daily and weekly overtime hours are not counted twice.

## [US-4] Meal period (California overlay)
An employee who works more than 5 hours must receive an unpaid meal period of at least 30 minutes that begins before the end of the fifth hour of work. A missed or late meal period is recorded and may require a premium payment of one additional hour of pay.

## [US-5] Recording attendance and missing punches
Every working period must have a clock-in and a matching clock-out. When a clock-out or clock-in is missing, the missing punch may be inferred from the scheduled shift time, within 30 minutes of the scheduled start or end, when no other evidence contradicts it. If there is no scheduled shift, the case must be referred to the manager for the actual time.

## [US-6] Late arrival and early departure
A clock-in more than 7 minutes after the scheduled start is a late arrival. A clock-out more than 7 minutes before the scheduled end is an early departure. Both are recorded as schedule deviations for manager review.

## [US-7] Overlapping attendance records
Worked periods for the same employee must not overlap in time. When two recorded shifts overlap, the duplicate or erroneous record may be voided or its punch adjusted only when the evidence shows which record is wrong. An overlap longer than 2 hours must be referred to the manager.

## [US-8] Accurate records of hours worked
All hours actually worked must be recorded and paid. A correction must never move or remove punches to hide overtime or a missed meal period that actually occurred.

## [US-9] Corrections and approval
Original punches are never edited. Each correction is a new record that states the reason, the approver, and the policy clause relied on. Pay-affecting corrections require explicit approval by an authorised manager.
