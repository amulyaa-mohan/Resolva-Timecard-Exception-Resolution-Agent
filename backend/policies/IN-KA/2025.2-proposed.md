# IN-KA Attendance Policy, version 2025.2-proposed (PROPOSED, not in force)

Simplified demonstration handbook for Resolva. Not legal advice.

## [KA-1] Scope
This policy applies to hourly employees working at establishments in Karnataka. It governs how attendance is recorded, how hours are counted, and how timecard corrections are approved.

## [KA-2] Daily working hours
Regular working time must not exceed 10 hours in a single day, and total working time including overtime must not exceed 12 hours. Time worked beyond 10 hours in a day is overtime.

## [KA-3] Weekly working hours
Regular working time must not exceed 48 hours in a week. Time worked beyond 48 hours in a week is overtime.

## [KA-4] Overtime pay
Overtime is paid at twice the ordinary rate of wages. Overtime must be recorded against the day on which it was worked.

## [KA-5] Quarterly overtime limit
Total overtime must not exceed 144 hours in any quarter. A correction must never move or remove punches to hide overtime that was actually worked.

## [KA-6] Rest interval
An employee must not work more than 5 hours continuously without a rest interval of at least 1 hour. The rest interval must be recorded with break punches.

## [KA-7] Spread-over
The spread-over, measured from the first clock-in to the last clock-out of a working day, must not exceed 12 hours including rest intervals. A spread-over breach must be reviewed by a manager and must not be corrected by moving punches without evidence of a punching error.

## [KA-8] Recording attendance and missing punches
Every working period must have a clock-in and a matching clock-out. When a clock-out or clock-in is missing, the missing punch may be inferred from the scheduled shift time, within 30 minutes of the scheduled start or end, when no other evidence contradicts it. If there is no scheduled shift, the case must be referred to the manager for the actual time.

## [KA-9] Late arrival and early departure
A clock-in more than 7 minutes after the scheduled start is a late arrival. A clock-out more than 7 minutes before the scheduled end is an early departure. Both are recorded as schedule deviations for manager review.

## [KA-10] Corrections and approval
Original punches are never edited. Each correction is a new record that states the reason, the approver, and the policy clause relied on. Pay-affecting corrections require explicit approval by an authorised manager.

## [KA-11] Overlapping attendance records
Worked periods for the same employee must not overlap in time. When two recorded shifts overlap, the duplicate or erroneous record may be voided or its punch adjusted only when the evidence shows which record is wrong. An overlap longer than 2 hours must be referred to the manager.
